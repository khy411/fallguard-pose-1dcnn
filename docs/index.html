FallGuard

Sees a fall, not a face.

FallGuard is an AI fall detector that works from a skeleton instead of a picture. Your camera feed turns into a stick figure inside this tab, and the video is never shown, saved, or sent.

Stand a few meters back so your body is in view, then act out a fall onto a bed or cushions. Never a hard floor.

Start the webcam or analyze a clip and your skeleton appears here.

Pose tracking by MediaPipe. The fall classifier is a small 1D-CNN I trained on a public fall dataset (GMDCSA-24) and my own recordings, and it runs in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web.

Prototype for one person in view. It can miss falls and raise false alarms, and it is not a medical device.
